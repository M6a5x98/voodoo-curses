Voodoo Curses allow the player to use voodoo magic through custom items and mechanics.

## Voodoo Dolls
By right-clicking on a player with it, you can bind the item to the target's soul. You can use pins with the doll.
## Pins
They exist in 2 type the **Blessing Pin** and the **Cursing Pin**. By using them while holding a compatible item (listed below) they will absorb the curse or blessing associated to this item
### Compatible Items :
#### Blessing
- **Clover** (Luck)
- **Cake** (Speed)
- **Netherite Pickaxe** (Haste)
- **Compacted Block of Netherite** (Full beacon effects)
#### Curse
- **Powder Snow Bucket** (Slowness)
- **Grindstone** (Target take damage if wearing enchants)
- **Blast Furnace** (Target take damage if wearing armor)
- **Compacted Block of Netherite** (Target take damage until on 1/2 heart)
## Clover
A rare item dropped by grass

## Profaned Block of Redstone
A profaned version of the redstone block crafted in a smithing table with a **Redstone Block**, a **Soul Sand** like block and a **Voodoo Ritual Cursing Template**
## Purified Block of Lapis
This lapis block has been purified through fire
